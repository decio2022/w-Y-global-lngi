# Global w-Y LNGI

## Time direction and progress

Set the time speed at the top of the page: positive values move forward,
negative values rewind, and zero stops the clock. Use Pause/Continue or click
the sequence to pause or resume. Rewinding stops at the start of the timeline.

In the Progress tab, **Follow time direction** makes the bars and their
click-to-jump targets follow a negative speed toward earlier boundaries. Click a
reverse bar to move just past its boundary and immediately show the next earlier
sequence and countdown; at the beginning, it stops at the start of the timeline.
Turn the option off to keep the original forward-looking bars. Reverse intervals
that share a target are shown once, and already-reached or precision-limited
targets are omitted (except for the start-of-timeline indicator). The displayed
countdowns and dates are in simulated time, not wall-clock time. The speed and
this option are saved locally.

Run the tests with `for test in tests/*.test.js; do node "$test"; done`.
